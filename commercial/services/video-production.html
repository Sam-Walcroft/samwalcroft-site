Video Production

End-to-end filming and editing on the Blackmagic Cinema Camera 6K — concept, shoot, colour grade, motion graphics and delivery, built for wherever the piece actually lands, not a one-size cut reused everywhere.

The Process
01
Concept & Scripting
Writing and scripting creative for the specific audience it's built for — a TVC funnel reads nothing like a music video brief, and the process starts by treating them differently.
02
Filming
Shot on the Blackmagic Cinema Camera 6K across ads, music videos, and social content — from single-setup product shoots to six-video days under real time pressure.
03
Editing & Colour
Full post-production in Adobe Premiere Pro, with colour grading handled in DaVinci Resolve for a genuinely graded look rather than a preset LUT.
04
Motion Graphics & VFX
After Effects work — medium VFX for narrative pieces, motion graphics for commercial ones — built to serve the story, not call attention to itself.
05
Delivery
Formatted for the platform it's actually landing on — a vertical TikTok cut and a horizontal TVC are never the same export.
